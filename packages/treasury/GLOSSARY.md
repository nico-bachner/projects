# Treasury

Reimburses Sydney University Canoe Club members for money they spent on the club, via Sydney Uni Sport.

## People

**Club**:
The Sydney University Canoe Club, whose members Treasury reimburses.
_Avoid_: SUCC, society

**SUS**:
Sydney Uni Sport, which pays out reimbursements.

**Member**:
A club member who can pay for club spending personally and claim it back.
_Avoid_: User, payer, person

**Signatory**:
The treasurer, president, vice president or secretary: the members who can approve claims.
_Avoid_: Approver, executive

**Treasurer**:
The one signatory whose approval every claim needs.

## Spending

**Receipt**:
The proof of one purchase, listing its line items.

**Vendor**:
Who the member bought from.

**Line item**:
One thing bought on a receipt, which may or may not be claimed.
_Avoid_: Item, entry, expense

**Category**:
What kind of club spending a claimed line item is, such as Gear or Trips.

**Discipline**:
A kind of paddling that a claimed line item's spending serves, such as Polo or Whitewater, or General for the whole club.
_Avoid_: N/A, none, all

**Claim**:
A member's ask to be reimbursed for line items from one or more receipts.
_Avoid_: Request

**Purpose**:
What a claim's spending was for, written by the member, such as "Polo training BBQ".
_Avoid_: Title, description

## Review

**Review**:
One signatory's decision on a claim: an approval, a change request or a rejection.

**Approval**:
A signatory's agreement that a claim should go to SUS.
_Avoid_: Sign-off

**Change request**:
A review objecting to a claim's details, which the member can fix.

**Rejection**:
A review objecting to the claim itself, which no fix would answer.

## Submission

**Batch**:
The approved claims sent to SUS together in one submission.

**Submission**:
Sending a batch to SUS for reimbursement.
